The Windsor at Westside Luxury Family Resort Offers Something for Everyone.

Experience the Best of Windsor at Westside Resort Amenities

When you book SK Magic Homes, you don't just get a stunning vacation home — you get full access to the exceptional Windsor at Westside Resort amenities that make this one of the most sought-after resort communities near Orlando and Walt Disney World. From a jaw-dropping resort pool complex to a Mediterranean-inspired clubhouse packed with activities for every age, Windsor at Westside has been designed to deliver a true resort vacation from the moment you arrive.

Resort-Style Pool & Water Features

The crown jewel of Windsor at Westside Resort amenities is the spectacular zero-entry resort pool complex. Guests of all ages can splash through the thrilling waterslide, drift along the relaxing lazy river, or soak in the bubbling hot tub after a long day at the theme parks. Families with young children will love the dedicated kids' splash water playground — a safe, shallow space designed just for little ones.

Surrounding the pool is generous sundecking with comfortable loungers, shaded parasols, and tables perfect for soaking up the Florida sunshine. Want a more private poolside experience? Reserve one of the premium private cabanas for your group and enjoy your own exclusive slice of paradise right at the water's edge.

The Clubhouse: Your Resort Hub

At the heart of the community sits the stunning 10,000 sq. ft. Mediterranean-themed clubhouse — a destination in itself. Whether you're looking to relax, play, or grab a bite, the clubhouse has you covered:

  • Tu Casa Tiki Bar & Lounge — Kick back with a frozen cocktail, cold beer, or glass of wine at the poolside tiki bar, complete with a cozy fireplace lounge. Food is available for convenient poolside dining so the fun never has to stop.
  • Free Arcade & Game Room — Keep the kids (and the adults) entertained with complimentary access to the video arcade and games room. No quarters needed.
  • Kids' Activity Center — A dedicated children's space stocked with board games, creative activities, and more to keep younger guests happily occupied.
  • Fitness Center — Stay on top of your workout routine with access to a well-equipped fitness facility during your stay.
  • Business & Computer Room — Need to check in with the office? A business center with computer access is available for guests.
  • Sundry Shop & Marketplace — Pick up last-minute essentials, snacks, and souvenirs without ever leaving the resort.
  • Free WiFi throughout the amenity area keeps the whole family connected.

Sports, Recreation & Outdoor Activities

Adventure doesn't stop at the pool. Windsor at Westside Resort amenities extend to a full lineup of outdoor recreation that will keep active guests busy throughout their stay:

  • Tennis courts and basketball courts for friendly competition
  • Sand volleyball court for a classic beach-style game
  • Soccer field for the sports lovers in your group
  • Multi-sports court for versatile outdoor play
  • Children's playground for the youngest guests
  • Pedal boats for leisurely fun on the water
  • Aqua Runner water obstacle course for thrill-seekers
  • Mermaid Academy — a unique and magical water experience kids absolutely love
  • Outdoor movie screenings — weekly film nights under the stars create unforgettable family memories

Security, Privacy & Peace of Mind

Windsor at Westside Resort operates with a 24-hour monitored gated entry, giving your family complete peace of mind throughout your stay. The resort is a private, secure community — so you can relax knowing your loved ones are safe at all times.
