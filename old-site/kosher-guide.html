At the Parks · 8 miles

Kosher Meals at Walt Disney World

You can eat kosher inside the parks with a little planning. Walt Disney World serves sealed, certified-kosher meals at most table-service restaurants and several quick-service locations across all four parks. The simplest way to arrange one is to call (407) WDW-DINE / 407-939-3463 and add a kosher meal to your dining reservation — ideally at least 24 hours ahead, though some locations can accommodate same-day.

Because the meals arrive sealed from a certified kosher vendor and are opened at your table, they can take a little longer to serve — worth knowing when you're managing a hungry group. Full details are on Disney's official special dietary requests page.

Good To Know

Kosher-Friendly Villa: FAQ

Is SK Magic Homes a kosher villa?

It's a kosher-friendly villa. The home is not rabbi-supervised and the kitchen is not certified or kashered, so it isn't a "kosher kitchen" in the formal sense. What it does offer is a Sabbath Mode refrigerator, a separate kosher-capable cooktop available on request, ample space to prepare and share meals, and kosher food and community nearby. Families keep to their own standard of kashrut, and I'm always glad to talk specifics before you book.

Does the kitchen have Sabbath Mode appliances?

Yes. The full-size refrigerator has a Sabbath Mode that holds its light and temperature behavior through Shabbat and Yom Tov, and the range's oven offers a Sabbath Mode as well — so you don't have to worry about interior lights or automatic shut-off cycles on either one.

Can I get kosher food delivered to the villa?

Yes. Kosher Kaddy delivers kosher groceries to the Kissimmee area, including our resort corridor, with no minimum order and free delivery over a set threshold. Kosher Eatery near Lake Buena Vista caters, which is an easy way to arrange a Shabbat or holiday meal. KosherOrlando.org lists additional grocery and prepared-food options.

How do I order kosher meals at Disney?

Call (407) WDW-DINE / 407-939-3463 and add a kosher meal to your dining reservation, ideally at least 24 hours ahead. Disney serves sealed, certified-kosher meals at most table-service restaurants and several quick-service spots across all four parks. See Disney's official special dietary requests page for the latest details.

Can you set up the cooktop or other kosher-friendly items?

A separate small electric cooktop is available on request — just ask before you arrive. If there's something specific that would make your stay easier, let me know when you book and I'll do my best to accommodate it.

How far is the nearest synagogue?

The nearest Chabad is Chabad of South Orlando on West Sand Lake Road, about twenty minutes north by car. The villa is not within walking distance of a synagogue, so many observant guests plan Shabbat at the home and save shul and dining out for the rest of the week.
