Frequently Asked Questions

Is SK Magic Homes owner managed?

Yes. SK Magic Homes SK Magic Homes is personally owner managed by your hosts to ensure guests receive attentive service and a consistently high-quality experience.  We do have two on-site managers who live in Windsor at Westside and can offer assistance with any needs during your stay.  You will communicate with both of us during the course of your reservation.  

How close is the villa to Disney?

I am always hesitant to give drive times, because they vary greatly depending upon what time of day you travel.  I highly recommend leaving SK Magic Homes early in the morning, or after the morning rush hour, if you want the best experience.  That being said, here are some distances and drive times for popular destinations:

  • Animal Kingdom – closest park – 8.7 miles – 20 minutes.
  • Magic Kingdom – 11.9 miles, 22 minutes
  • Epcot – 11.7 miles – 24 minutes
  • Disney Springs – 14.1 miles – 28 minutes
  • Hollywood Studios – 10.4 miles – 22 minutes
  • Epic Universe – 20 miles – 40 minutes
Is pool heat included?

Yes. Direct booking guests receive complimentary pool heat year-round.  We heat the pool to between 86-88 degrees.  We also provide propane free of charge for our Weber Genesis grill that is included for you to use.

Is the villa good for large families?

Absolutely. The home was specifically designed for large family vacations and group travel with spacious gathering areas and accommodations for up to 18 guests.

Why book directly instead of Airbnb?

Direct booking guests typically receive:

  • lower pricing
  • fewer fees
  • direct owner communication
  • complimentary pool heat
  • earlier booking access to holidays and busy times of year
  • a more personalized experience

We are superhosts on Airbnb, premier hosts on VRBO, and list on other sites as well.  If booking directly makes you nervous, we would still like you to book with us!  You can check out our linked listings to see them for yourself.
