Seeking a Magical Family Escape in the Heart of Disneyworld?

SK Magic Homes is your owner-managed Disneyworld villa near Walt Disney World Resort, designed to give families the ultimate Central Florida getaway just 20-25 minutes from the parks. Located in the highly sought-after Windsor at Westside Resort, this recently renovated Disneyworld villa combines resort-style amenities with the comfort, space, and personal touches of a true home-away-from-home.

Just a five-minute walk from the resort clubhouse, guests enjoy access to family-friendly amenities including a zero-entry pool with waterpark, lazy river, fully equipped fitness center, free arcade games, sports courts, and more. After a day at Disneyworld, relax in your own private screened pool and spa, unwind in the game room, or let the kids escape into the immersive Star Wars, Harry Potter, and Frozen-themed bedrooms they’ll be talking about long after vacation ends.

Perfect for Disney family vacations, multigenerational trips, and group getaways, SK Magic Homes offers spacious accommodations, stylish décor, and the convenience of being close to Orlando’s top attractions, restaurants, golf, and shopping. Whether you’re searching for a luxury Disneyworld villa with themed rooms and resort amenities or planning the perfect family trip to Disney, SK Magic Homes is the perfect place to create unforgettable vacation memories.

Set Up for Families Who Keep Kosher
